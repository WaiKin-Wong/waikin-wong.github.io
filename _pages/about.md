I obtained my PhD at the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, where I had the privilege of being advised by [Prof. Shuai Wang](https://www.cse.ust.hk/~shuaiw/). Currently, I am working as a Postdoc at HKUST. My research focuses on improving the dependability of software systems.
